Multispectral Image Recompression in Ciphertext Domain with Texture Block Decision and 3D-MDCT

2026-11-06

Remote sensing systems increasingly generate large volumes of multispectral imagery, creating a need for secure and efficient data handling. Traditional joint encryption and compression methods designed for natural images struggle with the specific demands of multispectral data, particularly in managing interband redundancy, capturing spatial texture variation, and preserving spectral consistency for downstream tasks. To address these limitations, we developed JECA-MS, a joint encryption and compression framework tailored for multispectral remote sensing images that supports recompression directly in the ciphertext domain.

Our approach integrates four core components. First, an adaptive two-size texture block decision strategy classifies image regions into strong and weak texture blocks, reducing data volume in weak-texture areas by up to fourfold. Second, we introduce a modified three-dimensional discrete cosine transform that improves spatial-spectral decorrelation, particularly in homogeneous regions like clouds and water. Third, a ciphertext domain recompression mechanism allows flexible adjustment of compression ratios without requiring decryption. Finally, we define a dedicated coding format to ensure efficient data encapsulation and compatibility with standard remote sensing structures.

Experimental results demonstrate that JECA-MS improves compression ratios by 55% for water images and 36% for cloud images, while reducing encoding and decoding times by 39% and 68% compared to existing methods. Security evaluations confirm that the framework resists statistical attacks and maintains a practical balance between lightweight encryption and compression efficiency. By enabling secure, flexible data management directly in the encrypted domain, this work provides a reliable foundation for processing multispectral remote sensing imagery in security-sensitive and resource-constrained environments.
