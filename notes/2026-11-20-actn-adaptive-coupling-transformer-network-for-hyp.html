ACTN: Adaptive Coupling Transformer Network for Hyperspectral Image Classification

2026-11-20

Hyperspectral image (HSI) classification remains a challenging task in remote sensing due to the complex spectral and spatial correlations inherent in the data. While convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and Transformer architectures have individually demonstrated strong performance, they typically focus on either local contextual features or global dependency relationships, respectively. This specialization often results in an incomplete capture of the multidimensional representations necessary for accurate classification. Furthermore, existing methods rarely fully exploit the complementary strengths of both architectural paradigms. To address these limitations, we propose the Adaptive Coupling Transformer Network (ACTN), a parallel-hybrid framework designed to enhance representation learning by facilitating mutual learning between local and global features.

The core of our approach is the Adaptive Coupling Module (ACM), which operates through two parallel branches. The CNN branch is responsible for extracting local contextual representations, while the Transformer branch captures global dependency structures. To effectively integrate these distinct feature sets, we introduce an Adaptive Response Fusion Module (ARFM). This module merges local and global representations at different resolutions in an adaptive manner, ensuring that the network can dynamically adjust to the varying information content within the HSI cube. Additionally, we employ a cosine similarity function to constrain the loss function during mutual learning. This mechanism guarantees the preservation of both local and global representations to the maximum extent, preventing the dominance of one feature type over the other.

We evaluated ACTN on three public HSI datasets. The experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches based on both Transformers and CNNs. This work is significant for the remote sensing community as it provides a robust framework for leveraging the complementary advantages of different deep learning architectures. By effectively coupling local and global representations, ACTN offers a more comprehensive understanding of hyperspectral data, which is crucial for advancing AI-driven applications in security monitoring, environmental assessment, and precision agriculture.

*Yang Xiaofei, Cao Weijia*, Tang Dong, Zhou Yicong, Lu Yao. ACTN: Adaptive Coupling Transformer Network for Hyperspectral Image Classification. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2025.* Paper
