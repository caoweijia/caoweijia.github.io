What happens when a map is out of date?

This synthetic grid illustrates how updating a map can change a planned route. It is a teaching demo with deterministic inputs.

Dark gray: known obstacle. Orange: newly observed obstacle. Blue: planned route. S/G: start/goal.

Simulation protocol

Both planners use breadth-first search on an 8 × 8 grid with four-directional movement and unit cost. The updated planner receives one newly observed obstacle when the map is outdated. Route clearance is checked against the actual grid. The comparison covers map updates only.
