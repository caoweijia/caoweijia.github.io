Weijia Cao

Associate Professor
Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences (AIRCAS)

I am an Associate Professor at the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Macau. My research focuses on trustworthy intelligence for remote sensing, with an emphasis on intelligent interpretation, data security, and privacy-preserving processing.

Research

Hyperspectral image analysisClassification and super-resolution of hyperspectral images with transformer, Mamba, frequency-domain and geometric representations, with a focus on models whose decisions can be inspected.
Remote sensing data securityprocessing in the ciphertext domain for multispectral remote sensing data.
Trustworthy AIFrameworks that let remote sensing data and models be shared and used under verifiable control, including privacy-preserving use of large models and onboard trustworthy intelligence.
World models for Earth observation
